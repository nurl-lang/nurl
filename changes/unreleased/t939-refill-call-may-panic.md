### Fixed

- **A field refilled by a call that may panic is refused** (AGORA-939). After `( take . h buf )` on an `inout` parameter, `= . h buf ( make_buf k )` runs `make_buf` before the store. If it panics, a `recover`ing caller gets `h` back with `buf` empty, and its next push read a null handle. The compiler now knows which calls cannot panic: it checks the whole module once every function is compiled. The refill is refused only when its call may panic, directly or through a function it calls, or when it calls a closure value. The common `= . h buf ( vec_new [u] )`, and a refill by any function that cannot panic, stay accepted. No source change is needed for those. For a refill that may panic, give the field a value first that no panic can stop, then make the new one. A module with no such refill pays nothing for the check.
