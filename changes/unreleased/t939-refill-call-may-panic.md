### Fixed

- **A field taken by, or refilled by, a call that may panic is refused** (AGORA-939). After `( take . h buf )` on an `inout` parameter, `= . h buf ( make_buf k )` runs `make_buf` before the store. If it panics, a `recover`ing caller gets `h` back with `buf` empty, and its next push read a null handle. The compiler now knows which calls cannot panic: it checks the whole module once every function is compiled. The refill is refused only when its call may panic, directly or through a function it calls, or when it calls a closure value. The common `= . h buf ( vec_new [u] )`, and a refill by any function that cannot panic, stay accepted. No source change is needed for those. For a refill that may panic, give the field a value first that no panic can stop, then make the new one. The same holds for the sink call that takes the field: `( take . h buf k )` hands the field over as the call starts, so a `take` that may panic is refused, and one that cannot panic stays accepted. A later call of the same statement that may unwind is refused too (`( add ( take . h buf ) ( mayp k ) )`), while a call in the sink's own arguments, which runs before the field is handed over, is not. For such a call, hand it a copy (`mem_dup`) and keep the field. A module with no such refill or take pays nothing for the check. A value whose `% Drop` panics at its scope's end between the take and the refill unwinds where no call does, and is not refused yet: that stays open as AGORA-942 (docs/LIMITATIONS.md).
