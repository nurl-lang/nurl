### Changed

- **The formal proof has an encoding of the hand-over of an owner's old value to the binding that reads it by name** (AGORA-885). After `= o other`, a later `= other …` gives `other`'s old value to `o` instead of freeing it, if `o` still reads that value then (it was not given another one in between). `formal/A2/HandOver.lean` reads that as an owner cell and a borrow, the hand-over being a `take` into the cell. It proves two encoded programs memory-safe and leak-free with A2's existing rules: a straight-line one, and one where the hand-over is decided at run time, for every value of the condition (`formal/A2/HANDOVER_DESIGN.md`). A general translation of nurlc's hand-overs into the encoding is phase C. A field's reader and the keep rule (`= o ( f … o … )`) stay outside the claim, as `formal/CLAIM.md` says.
