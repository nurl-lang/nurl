### Changed

- **The formal proof covers a call that hands a view to a function pushing through a field of its `inout` parameter** (AGORA-909). In A2, a field or payload read of the function's own binding may now be fresh, as an element borrow may: an owner or an `inout` parameter, or a fresh read of one, of a type that holds no views and that holds nothing. So `( vec_push . b items x )` and `( vec_push . . o bag items x )` in a function taking `inout` `b` or `o` no longer make it through-changing, and a call with a view of another value, read after the call, is typed, memory safe and leak-free (`A2/FreshProjWitness.lean`, `A2/FreshNestWitness.lean`). A read of a borrowed parameter, of a borrow that is not fresh, or of a type that holds views is never fresh. nurlc already accepted these programs, and NURL source is unchanged.
