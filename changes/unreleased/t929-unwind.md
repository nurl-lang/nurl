### Fixed

- **A call that may panic while an `inout` parameter's field is taken is refused** (AGORA-929). `( take . h buf )` followed by a call that may panic, before `= . h buf …` gives the field a value again, handed `h` back to a `recover`ing caller with the field empty. The caller's next push read a null handle. It is refused now: give the field a value before the call. The refill's own call, `= . h buf ( vec_new [u] )`, is not counted. **Breaking:** freeing a field by hand right before it is made again, as in `( vec_free [u] . it msg )` followed by a call and then `= . it msg …`, is refused when `it` is `inout`. Migration: drop the hand free, because the `=` releases the old value once the new one is made. nwasm 3.1.6, nurl-cov 0.3.2 and gRPC 0.3.2 are migrated.
- **A closure's own `inout` parameter can no longer go back with a field a sink took** (AGORA-929). `\ inout Holder x → v { : i n ( take . x buf ) }` left the caller's `h.buf` empty, and the next push read a null handle. It is refused now, as for a function's `inout` parameter.
- **A join's arm can no longer hand on a holder whose field a sink took** (AGORA-929). `^ ? c h h2` after `( take . h buf )` returned `h` with the field empty. It is refused now, as `= h2 h` already was.
- **A sink can no longer take a field that the struct's own `% Drop` reads** (AGORA-929). `( eat . h s )` on a `Named` whose destructor reads `. c s` left the destructor reading the emptied field when `h` ended. This holds for a nested field too (`. . o m s`). It is refused now. A field the destructor never reads can still be taken. With this, AGORA-280 has no open shapes left.
