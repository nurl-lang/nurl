### Changed

- **Growing one element lent by a call no longer ends a view of its container's own block** (AGORA-374, docs/MEMORY.md §2.10). `( string_push_str ( vec_at vs 0 ) … )` changes what is inside element 0, and `vs`'s block keeps its address and length. So a `Slice` of `vs` read after the call stays valid, as it does through a forwarder of your own or in a loop that grows each element in turn bounded by a slice of `vs`. Before, every one was refused ("a value lent from it was passed to 'string_push_str', which may move or free its buffer"). A view of any element still ends, the grown one's and its siblings' alike: the formal rules (A2) do not tell elements apart, and nurlc accepts no program they do not type here. New tests `elem_lend_grow_keeps_other_views`, `diag_borrow_elem_lend_grow_ends_another_elements_view`.
