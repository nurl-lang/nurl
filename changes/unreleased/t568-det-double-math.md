### Changed

- **`float_exp` and `float_log` give the same bits on every host** (AGORA-568). They called the host's libm, and glibc, UCRT, MinGW and macOS round e^x and ln x differently, so a program, a `grad` tape built on them, or a training run gave other numbers on another OS. They are now NURL's own: `stdlib/nurl_detmath.h`, a port of musl's table-driven `exp` and `log` (the algorithms glibc ships) on the paths that use no fma, built so no compiler fuses them into one. Same input, same bits, on Linux, Windows and macOS. They stay within 0.507 and 0.517 ulp of the exact result (4 million samples each against a 64-bit-mantissa reference), and agree with glibc's on all but 0.04 % (exp) and 0.007 % (log) of those inputs. The raw `exp` and `log` that std/float.nu declares stay the host's. Speed: `float_exp` costs the same as glibc's `exp` on Linux and is 2.6× faster with the MinGW runtime that installed Windows toolchains link (3.9 ns against 10.3 ns); `float_log` is equal on Windows and about 0.6 ns slower than glibc's fma version on Linux (4.1 ns against 3.5 ns), the price of a result that does not depend on the host's fma.
