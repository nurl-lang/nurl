### Added

- **The memory-model proof covers taking an element that holds views out through a borrow** (AGORA-897). An example is a slice popped through `vec_at vv 0` of a `Vec` of `Vec`s of slices and kept: `: [s y ( vec_pop b )`. The new rule `StmtT.takeBV` types AGORA-889's move-out step for element types that hold views and no closure, as a truncation through the borrow that also ends the views among the owners' elements. The new binding holds what the borrow holds, and the proof shows that the element's views stay valid through it. The soundness and leak-freedom theorems cover it, with no axiom and no `sorry`. A witness program (`formal/A2/TakeBVWitness.lean`) moves two views of a String's buffer out through a borrow, keeps and reads both, and is proven safe and leak-free. Element types that hold views and a closure, or a destructor value, stay outside the claim (`formal/CLAIM.md`).
