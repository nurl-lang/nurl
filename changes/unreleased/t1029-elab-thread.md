### Added

- **The kernel builds the certificate's term for programs that spawn threads too** (AGORA-1029 step 10). `C.Elab.elabIR3` (`formal/C/Elab.lean`) builds A3's term of a program that spawns: the program, its spawn wrappers and its entry (`elabIR3 ir = some (P, spawns, entry)`, `elabIs3_sound`). It follows each spawned closure back to its literal (the translator's spawn scan), types it at class `send` with an interface of its own, makes a spawn a call of its code's wrapper (its result the ok variant around a handle) and a join a read of the handle, reads a result `{ i1, T, E }` as `.sum E T` (its error by `.getl`) and a thread's handle and error as numbers.
  - All 4 thread programs of the 62 certify programs are now in the subset: every certify program is (58 before). The 2 the checker accepts get `Elab.lean` and `Cert.elab` (36 bundles, 34 before), whose statement is about `elabIR3`. No certify bundle trusts the translator for its term any more.
  - `elabIR3` stays stricter than the translator, never laxer (and `elabIR`, A2's, still refuses every program that spawns): it makes the translator's checks (a spawned closure used again after its spawn or in a loop around it, capturing anything by name, a `%ThreadErr` carrying more than numbers, a result's literal, a code spawned at two closure types or never), and it also refuses a use of a spawned closure in any word of any line, two spawned closure types in one function, and a result or a thread's handle inside another type. Each check has a kernel witness for its refusal and for its well-formed neighbour, and `elab_thread` builds `thread_spawned_with_a_number_capture`'s term.
  - `tools/ncore/elab_parity.sh` finds 0 differences and 0 programs `elabIR` takes but the translator refuses, over 263 programs in the subset (254 before). 5 new fixtures in `tools/ncore/elab/` (`thread_*`) cover the new rules.
  - `nurl-cert` 0.4.0: a threaded bundle's `Elab.lean` is about `C.Elab.elabIR3`; the skip before Lean (`ELAB_CTORS`) takes `.getl .send`.
  - Certify takes 80.9 s over the 62 certify programs against 76.5 s before (+5.7 %, best of 3 alternated runs against step 9): the 4 thread programs new to the subset take +0.85 to +1.41 s each for their Elab.lean part (2 of them the checker refuses, and spend it too), the other 58 the same (+0.34 s in all, within noise). The largest peak memory is 2487 MiB against 2513 MiB; a thread program's peak is +18 to +70 MiB. This is the price of the kernel building the 4 programs' terms: every certify bundle is out of the translator's trust now.
