### Changed

- **The formal proof's call lift now works with parameter regions** (AGORA-937, stage 2b). Every lift lemma behind `StmtT.ren` is stated and proved without assuming that no callee binding is a region: expressions, right-hand sides, calls, edits and takes through a borrow, the exits, and the context facts they use. The lift's substitution keeps a name and respects a weakening (`subE_weaker`). A region's index is always bound (an `AbsOK` clause), and the lift lemmas ask only that the statement names no region (its variables are below `nvars`) and that the callee context is fresh-sound (`FreshOK`, from the signature). No typing rule, theorem statement or semantics changes, and no program's typing changes yet: regions are turned on in a later change. NURL source is unchanged. Since no region is ever changed by name (`name_kill_not_region`), the lift's substitution drops a name dependency on a region, and the call setting's no-region assumption `RSetup.OK.noPi` is gone.
