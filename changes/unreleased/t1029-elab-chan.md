### Added

- **The kernel builds the certificate's term for programs with channels too** (AGORA-1029 step 9). `C.Elab.elabIR` (`formal/C/Elab.lean`) now also covers channels of numbers, Strings and Vecs of numbers (A2's `.lk (.prod (.vec T) .int)`), in a program that spawns no thread: `chan_new` (`.lockNew` of a fresh queue and a 0 flag), `Channel_share` (`.lockClone`), `chan_send` (`.chanSend`), `chan_recv` and `chan_try_recv` (`.chanRecv`, an owned option) and `chan_close` (`.chanClose`).
  - All 3 channel programs of the 62 certify programs are now in the subset (58 in all, 55 before). The 2 the checker accepts get `Elab.lean` and `Cert.elab` (34 bundles, 32 before). Outside stay the 4 thread programs, and nothing else.
  - A `ty` line whose drops say `d` for a type with no destructor of the program's (a library's: `%RawBlock`, `%MutexImpl`, which `stdlib/std/channel.nu` brings) no longer keeps a program out of the subset (step 8 refused it whole, stricter than the translator); a value of such a type is refused, as the translator refuses it.
  - `elabIR` stays stricter than the translator, never laxer. It makes the translator's checks (a channel of raw text, of a struct or of a channel; a channel call on a literal; a `chan_new` given an argument). It also refuses a channel inside another type or a closure type, and a channel element named by one letter. Each check has a kernel witness for its refusal and for its well-formed neighbour, and `elab_chan` builds `chan_int_sent_and_received`'s term.
  - `tools/ncore/elab_parity.sh` finds 0 differences and 0 programs `elabIR` takes but the translator refuses, over 254 programs in the subset (243 before). 7 new fixtures in `tools/ncore/elab/` (`chan_*`) cover the new rules.
  - `nurl-cert` 0.4.0: the skip before Lean (`ELAB_CTORS`) takes `.lockNew .lockClone .chanSend .chanRecv .chanClose .lk`. The selftest's program outside the subset, and its tamper bundle, is a thread program now (`thread_spawned_with_a_number_capture`).
  - Certify takes 76.5 s over the 62 certify programs against 76.8 s before (−0.3 %, best of 3 alternated runs against step 8, both formal/ trees built from scratch). The 3 channel programs new to the subset take +0.47 to +0.75 s each for their Elab.lean part (1 of them the checker refuses, and spends it too); the other 59 are 35 ms faster each, since Lean starts without `lake env` (below). `dOnlyOf` reads the `ty` lines in one pass. The largest peak memory is 2513 MiB against 2618 MiB, and the sum of the peaks is −12.8 %: `lake` no longer waits in every certify's process tree while Lean runs.

### Changed

- **Breaking (nurl-cert): `--lake PATH` / `$LAKE` is `--lean PATH` / `$LEAN`** (AGORA-1029 step 9). Certify and verify run `lean --json` inside `formal/` with `LEAN_PATH` set to `formal/`'s build, not `lake env lean` (≈ 45 ms a run before Lean started, ≈ 2.8 s over the 62 certify programs). elan's `lean`, run inside `formal/`, picks `formal/`'s toolchain as its `lake` did. Migration: pass `--lean` where you passed `--lake`.
