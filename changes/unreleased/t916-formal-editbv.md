### Changed

- **The formal proof covers a destructor of a type that holds views when it stores a view of its own local through a borrow of its own local** (Agora #916). Before, A2 typed every store of a view through a borrow as a change that may reach the caller's memory, so such a destructor was outside the claim. A2 now lets a view of a local `Vec` be fresh (it reaches only that `Vec`'s buffer) and held-fresh (it holds nothing an argument does), and types a store of such a view through a fresh borrow as one that changes only the destructor's own memory. Take a `% Drop` of `: V { ( Slice i ) s i k }` that pushes `( slice_from_vec w )` of a local `Vec i` into `( vec_at loc 0 )` of a local `Vec ( Vec ( Slice i ) )`. nurlc accepts it, it runs clean, and `A2/StoreViewWitness.lean` types it, memory safe and leak-free. This changes the calculus only; NURL source is unchanged. `formal/CLAIM.md` moves the case into the claim. A store of a view of a borrowed parameter stays outside: what it holds is the caller's memory.
