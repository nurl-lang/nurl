What this is

Hold a conversation by voice over the same pubkey-addressed mesh the rest of NURL's distributed stack runs on. This page embeds a small NURL program compiled to wasm32-wasi that reads your microphone and draws a live VU meter and frequency spectrum — so you can see your voice being captured before it is encoded and sent.

How the voice path works

• Peers are public keys, not IP addresses — the transport finds a direct peer-to-peer path when it can punch through NAT, and falls back to a relay only when it must.
• Audio is Opus, 48 kHz mono in 20 ms frames — pushed to one peer (unicast) or to every member of the channel (group multicast).
• Membership and presence converge with SWIM + CRDTs — no central server, no consensus, no coordination.

Channel. For this demo everyone shares one public channel. In the mobile app the channel will be a generated secret you share by QR code or link — only invited devices can join and decrypt it.

The embedded module

pptchat.nu is ~90 lines of NURL: it opens a canvas, polls the mic through the audio FFI each frame, and paints the meter. The networking layer (Opus + overlay transport) is the native build today; this first web slice focuses on capture.

Microphone access needs a secure context — open this page via https:// or http://localhost. Audio never leaves your browser in this demo.
