**agora #960: a closure stored in an `Rc`'s payload, run by a helper
handed the `Rc`.** The walk follows a closure into a struct, an option,
a Vec or a Box, but not into an Rc's payload. So `( runit hr )`, with `hr`
an `( Rc H )` whose `H` holds a closure that replaces another Rc's payload,
and `runit` running it through `rc_get`, ends no view of that payload.
The view then reads freed memory. Until the fix ships, do not hold a
view of an Rc payload across a call handed an Rc whose payload holds
closures.
