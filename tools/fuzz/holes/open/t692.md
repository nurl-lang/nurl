**agora #692: a `% Drop` value from a lending call, handed to a changer.** `( rg ( vec_at [R] rs 0 ) )`, where `R` is a struct with its own `% Drop` and `rg` grows a Vec field of its parameter, is not traced back to `rs`: a view of the element's buffer taken before the call is read after it (freed memory), a helper doing it has no summary edge, and neither thread rule sees it (eight threads growing one Vec in an Arc or a shared closure: double free). Without the `% Drop` impl every form is refused. Until the fix ships, bind the element first (`: R e ( vec_at [R] rs 0 )`, then `( rg e )`): the bound form is checked.
