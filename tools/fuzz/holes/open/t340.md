**agora #340: a `% Drop` holder nested nine or more levels deep.** The
check whether a value's drop may read a view stops at depth 8
(`__drop_may_read_view`), and below that it answers no. So a value whose
`% Drop` type sits nine or more layers of structs, Vecs, Boxes or
options down can be given a view of a binding declared after it, without
the younger-view refusal, and its destructor reads freed memory where
the scope ends. Until the fix ships, keep a `% Drop` type that holds a
view within eight layers of its binding, or declare the view's source
before the holder.
