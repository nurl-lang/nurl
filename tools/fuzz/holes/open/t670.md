**agora #670: a `^` or a `\` above a loop body's `;` skips the defer an earlier round armed.** A defer inside a loop body runs once, at function exit, if its `;` was reached in any round; a `^` or a failing `\` written above that `;` in the loop body leaves without running it, though an earlier round armed it (wrong output: the cleanup the defer does is not done on that way out; nothing is read after it is freed). Until the fix ships, register the defer outside the loop, above it, or leave the loop with `break` and return below it.
