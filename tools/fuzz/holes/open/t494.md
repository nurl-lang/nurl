**agora #494: a container handed to a `sink` parameter together with a borrow of its element.** `( eat_then_read t e )`, where `eat_then_read` takes `t` as `sink` and frees it, and `e` is `( vec_at [String] t 0 )` (or a closure over `e`, or over a `~` loop's element of `t`): the callee reads `e` after it freed `t` (heap-use-after-free). The exclusive-call check does not count a `sink` argument as one whose elements the callee drops. Until the fix ships, read the borrow before the call, or pass a copy (`string_from`, `mem_dup`) beside the container.
