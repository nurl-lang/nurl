**agora #444: a capture a closure reads on some path before assigning it on another.** A closure made after its capture's borrow ended is refused where it is made when its body reads the capture before assigning it. The scan is textual, so a read reached only past an assignment in a branch, a loop, a nested closure, `recover`, or a sibling field's assignment is missed, and calling the closure reads freed memory. Until the fix ships, don't capture a binding whose borrow has ended, even to assign it.
