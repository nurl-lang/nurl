**agora #373: a `vec_get` binding kept across a call that moves the
elements.** `: ?String o ( vec_get [String] vs 0 )`, then `( vec_swap
[String] vs 0 1 )` or `( vec_insert [String] vs 0 … )`, then `o`'s
payload handed back: the hand-back takes the element out through the slot
the binding was bound from, which now holds another element. The caller
gets that one as owned while the Vec still drops it, and reads freed
memory. Until the fix ships, take what you need out of a `vec_get`
binding before swapping, inserting or removing elements of its Vec, or
copy it (`mem_dup`).
