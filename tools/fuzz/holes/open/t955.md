**agora #955: a `:` join's field borrow assigned to a closure's capture by name.** Inside a closure, `: x . p s`, then `: y ? c x ( mk )` (or `? c . p s ( mk )`) and `= t y`, with `t` an outer binding the closure captures by name and `p` a local of the closure body, leaves `t` holding the struct's field after the body released it: a heap-use-after-free when `t` is read after the call. Outside a closure the same shape is refused. Until the fix ships, hand the value to the capture directly (`= t ? c x ( mk )`, which copies it) or read it inside the closure.
