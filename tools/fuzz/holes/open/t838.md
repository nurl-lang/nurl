**agora #838: a returned closure's effects are lost through a closure literal that captures it, or a closure literal's call.** A builder's closure assigned in an arm or copied, then captured by a literal and run; a closure literal's call handing back a builder's closure it captured. Running it after taking a view of what it changes leaves the view dangling (heap-use-after-free). (Through a plain pass-through call, `( pick ( mk xs ) other )`, a forwarder or a generic identity `( idg [( @ v )] ( mk xs ) )`, it is refused since agora #813.) Until the fix ships, run a closure a builder hands back from the binding it is bound to, or take the view after running it.
