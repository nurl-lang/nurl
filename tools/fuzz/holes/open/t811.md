**agora #811: a `~` loop element of a parameter's Vec handed to a callee that clears through it; a struct holding a closure that captured a share.** A function looping `~ w ws { ( f w ) }` over its parameter, `f` clearing the payload of `w`'s share, reports no drop of the parameter; and a struct whose field is a closure that captured an `Rc` share reaches no `Rc` type. Either way a caller's view through another handle outlives the call (heap-use-after-free). Until the fix ships, take such views after the call.
