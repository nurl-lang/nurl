**agora #811: a struct holding a closure that captured a share, run through a dyn method.** A struct whose field is a closure that captured an `Rc` share reaches no `Rc` type when the closure runs through a `dyn` method of an impl holding it. A caller's view through another handle then outlives the call (heap-use-after-free). The loop-element shapes are closed (AGORA-495), and so are the static-method and callee shapes. Until the fix ships, take such views after the call.
