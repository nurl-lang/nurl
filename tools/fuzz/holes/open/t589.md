**agora #589: a container grown through an alias the walk loses.** A `: ~ ( Vec T ) cur t` cursor of `t` that reaches a growth through a `?` / `??` join bound to a name (`: c ? k cur other`), as a second parameter beside `t` (`( walk t cur )`), captured by a closure a callee runs or that holds the loop itself, copied into a struct literal (`@ H { cur 0 }`), or as an Rc clone handed to a helper, moves `t`'s buffer under a `~` loop over `t` or a view of it (heap-use-after-free); the direct spellings are refused. Until the fix ships, grow a container only by its own name or a plain cursor of it while a loop or a view of it is live.
