**agora #448: a closure body's local bound from its view parameter through a join, stored into a capture.** `: s t ? c p `x`` followed by `= w @ V { t }`, with `w` a capture, gives `w` a view of the parameter that the caller's String may outlive only briefly. The direct and plainly bound forms are refused (agora #312, #332). Until the fix ships, store a copy that owns its data (string_from).
