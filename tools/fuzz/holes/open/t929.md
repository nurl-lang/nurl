**agora #929: a field a `sink` took, reached by two remaining routes.** A join's arm written as a block, `^ ? c { h } h2` after `( take . h buf )`, hands `h` on with the field empty, where `^ ? c h h2` is refused. And in an `inout` function, `: b . h buf` followed by `( take b )` takes the field out of `h` through the binding, so `h` goes back to its caller empty, where `( take . h buf )` is refused. The caller's next push reads a null handle. Until the fix ships, write the arm as the binding itself (`? c h h2`), and hand the field to the sink directly (`( take . h buf )`), so the check sees it.
