**agora #413: a loop whose body gives an outer owner a value and then
moves it.** With `( string_free x )` after `= x …` in the body, the
loop's verification walk runs, and what a holder depended on when the
loop was entered (or what a `continue` brought to the head) is lost at
the loop's exit: a zero-trip loop leaves `a` viewing a String that ends,
and the read after it is of freed memory. Until the fix ships, don't
read a holder after such a loop if a source it viewed before the loop
may have ended; give the holder a new value first.
