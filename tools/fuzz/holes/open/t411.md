**agora #411: a view of a match payload's field, held past the arm
through the payload's owner.** `: s w ( name_of oh )` with `name_of`
returning `( string_data . p name )` from its own `??`, or a view of a
struct owner's field kept while a copy of its handle grows that field
(`: ~ H q hs  ( string_push_str . q name … )`): a change of the payload
made in place through a second match, an `inout` callee's own match, or a
handle copy does not end the views of the owner's fields, so a later read
is of freed memory. Until the fix ships, don't change an option's payload
or a struct's field in place while a view of that field is still read.
