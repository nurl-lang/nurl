**agora #678: a wrapped lending call binds no borrow.** A binding whose right-hand side is a lending call (`vec_at`, `vec_get`, any function that hands back a parameter's element or field) written over more than one line - an argument on a later line than the `:` head, or the whole call on the next line - takes no borrow from the call's arguments, because the pending lend is matched to the binding by line number; ending the source afterwards (`vec_clear`, a push that grows it, a drop) is accepted and the binding reads freed memory. The same statement on one line is refused. Until the fix ships, keep a binding and its lending call on a single line, or clone the value.
