**agora #842: an option parameter's payload grown inline by its callee, a slice of a payload handed to a growing call with its option, an enum payload's element kept past the enum.** Three shapes stay open:
- a callee growing `( vec_at q 0 )`, `( rc_get p )` or `( box_get p )` inline through its option parameter's payload is in no summary;
- `( wr o sl )`, with `sl` a slice of `o`'s payload and `wr` growing it;
- a view out of a `??` arm over an enum bound by `:`, kept past the enum being moved or sunk.

Each is a heap-use-after-free. A view of a local option's payload handed out of its arm is closed (AGORA-842 part 1). Until the fix ships, bind an element before growing it through an option parameter, and use a payload view inside its arm only.
