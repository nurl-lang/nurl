**agora #690: an ArcWeak crosses a thread where its Arc may not.** `( ArcWeak T )` captured by a `thread_spawn` closure is accepted when `T` is Send but not Sync (a `PNCounter`, a `Cell`-holding struct), where `( Arc T )` is refused: the Send/Sync walk asks the harder question below an `Arc` and an `RwLocked` only. `arc_weak_upgrade` on the other thread then yields an `( Arc T )` on the same payload, and two threads change one unsynchronised state (double free under ASan). Until the fix ships, send an `ArcWeak` to another thread only when its payload could be shared through an `Arc`.
