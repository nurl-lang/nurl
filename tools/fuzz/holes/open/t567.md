**agora #567: an `inout` parameter handed to a `sink`.** `@ f inout ( Vec S ) p → i { ^ ( eat p ) }` called with a field target, `( f . h a )`: the callee frees the field, and the struct drops it again (heap-use-after-free). Through a join, `( eat ? c p p )`, the parameter is copied for the callee while the caller takes it for consumed, and the original leaks. Until the fix ships, pass a whole local to such a function, and hand the parameter to the sink by name.
