**agora #309: a view of a block-local struct's field, kept in an outer
value.** `= w @ W { ( string_data . h name ) }` with `h` a struct local
to an inner block, or the same view pushed into an outer container or
handed out through an `inout` callee: after the block, a read of `w`
reads the freed String. An `inout` callee that assigns its struct of
views whole, to a value viewing its own local, does the same, where a
field store is refused. Until the fix ships, keep a view of a struct's
field only while the struct itself is in scope, or copy the field out
(`string_from`).
