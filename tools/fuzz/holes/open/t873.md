**agora #873: an owner given back itself forgets what it borrows.** After `= o ( id other )`, a `= o ( id o )` (a call that may hand back o's own value) drops what `o` borrowed from `other`, so `= other …` is not refused and `o` then reads freed memory. Until the fix ships, do not hand a binding that borrows another back to itself through a call; copy it first.
