**agora #601: a cursor of a container that reaches a growth through a struct literal, or an Rc clone handed to a helper.** With `: ~ ( Vec T ) cur t`, growing through `. q items` after `: ~ H q @ H { cur 0 }`, or through a helper handed a clone of the Rc that holds `t`, moves `t`'s buffer under a `~` loop over `t` or a view of it (heap-use-after-free); the direct spellings — the cursor itself, a join of cursors, a second parameter, a closure over it — are refused. Until the fix ships, grow a container only by its own name or a plain cursor of it while a loop or a view of it is live.
