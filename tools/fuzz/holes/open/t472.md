**agora #472: an iterator yields copies of what it captured.** `( iter_collect [s] ( iter_from_vec [s] t ) )`, `iter_find` or `iter_fold` over it, `iter_map` collected, a direct `( it 0 )`, or a function of your own draining a closure parameter hands back the views the iterated Vec held with no tie to the string they view, which can be freed under them. Until the fix ships, iterate with `~ x t` or `vec_at`, or keep the strings alive past the result.
