**agora #707: a call's result passed straight as an argument, when the callee panics.** `( boom ( bomb `temp` 2 ) 1 )` under `recover`, where `bomb` returns a struct with its own `% Drop` and `boom` panics: the temporary is the caller's to release after the call, and it is not in the panic journal for that window, so the unwind never runs its destructor and what it owns leaks. A leak, not a fault. Bound first (`: Bomb p ( bomb … )  ( boom p 1 )`) it is dropped. Until the fix ships, bind such an argument before the call when the callee can panic.
