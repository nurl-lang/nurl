**agora #446: handing back a lend of a value that cannot be copied, through a rebinding, a generic identity, a payload, a capture's field or join, or the capture itself.** A closure's `^ c` of `( vec_at cs 0 )` is refused, but `: d c ^ d`, `( id ( vec_at cs 0 ) )` bound first, a `vec_get` payload of a capture's Vec, a captured struct's field, a join over a capture's element, or a `% Drop` capture itself handed back are not, and the value is dropped twice. Named functions share the rebinding and payload forms. Until the fix ships, hand back a value the body takes out of its owner (vec_remove) or makes.
