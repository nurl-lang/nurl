**agora #242: a closure bound by `:` from a join or a value block and then
handed on by name, in one remaining shape.** The other shapes are closed:
- a `:` binding of a join takes the owner its arm selects, and keeps how deep
  what its other arms lend lives, so handed to an outer binding it is copied
  there or refused (AGORA-953);
- a non-copyable one handed back is refused where its join's calls lend this
  frame's values (AGORA-953);
- a value block's raw view of its own local gets a copy, the type written or
  not, and `= a g` with g a
  second name of a's own env releases nothing (AGORA-953);
- an `inout` parameter takes a binding owned per path, and a block ending in
  a fresh call owns its result;
- a value a `;` defer reads or releases is refused, and so is a `:` join's
  arm reading a field of an inner block's struct, assigned outward
  (AGORA-941);
- a `??` whose arms are closures over a local option's payload, bound and
  handed back or assigned outward, is refused as a closure over a local is
  (AGORA-958).

One shape still faults, the elaborated twin of a closed probe
([`twins.txt`](twins.txt)): a join of a closure's locals' lends, bound by `:`
in the closure and assigned to a capture by name, does not carry what it
captures to the binding.

Until #222 closes them, hand such a closure on directly (`^ ?? o { … }`)
instead of through a binding.
