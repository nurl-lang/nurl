**agora #498: a `: ~` cursor bound to a `?` join whose arms are owners or their fields.** `: ~ ( Vec Nd ) c ? own . u kids . t kids` and then `( vec_clear [Nd] c )` ends no borrow of either arm, so `: Nd e ( vec_at [Nd] . t kids 0 )` taken before it is read after its storage was freed (heap-use-after-free). The same holds for a join of a struct owner (`: ~ H hc ? flag h g`, then a clear of `. hc items`) and of a loop element's field. A join whose arms are borrows is refused. Until the fix ships, clear the chosen owner by its own name in each branch (`? own { ( vec_clear … . u kids ) } { ( vec_clear … . t kids ) }`) rather than through a join cursor.
