**agora #545: an owned slice moved on, given a parameter, or read after a move.** A slice binding that owns its buffer (`: [i q ( mk )`, `: ~ [i d [i | 1 2]`) and is then handed to an aggregate (`^ @ Box { Full q }`, `^ @ Holder { q 1 }`) or copied (`: [i r q  ^ r`) is released by the binding and by its new holder (heap-use-after-free). `= d p` with p a parameter keeps d owning, and d's drop frees the caller's buffer. After `= e a`, a can still be read, and the next `= e [ … ]` frees it under that read. Through an `inout [i` parameter, `= p q` or `= p [ … ]` releases a buffer twice. Until the fix ships, keep a buffer that is handed on in a `Vec` rather than a `[T` slice, and never assign a parameter, or another binding you still read, to an owned slice binding.
