**agora #451: a `?` or `??` join of two owner bindings handed straight
to a `sink` parameter.** `( eat ? c u t )` with `u` and `t` owned
Strings: the owner the join picked is moved into the callee and still
dropped at its own scope's end, so its buffer is freed twice or read
after it is freed. Until the fix ships, bind the join first (`: String
j ? c u t  ( eat j )`) and hand the binding over.
