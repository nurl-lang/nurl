**agora #870: a borrowed copy's field handed over by the store that replaces it.** A function given a borrowed copy of a struct (`t[*]`) that moves a field of it into another binding by the `=` that gives the field a new value (`= out . s body` then `= . s body …`, with no `unsafe` write-back of `s` into its slot) leaves the slot and the result owning one body: heap-use-after-free in the caller. The walk accepts it; the ownership engine (`nurlc --engine-decides`) refuses it. Until the fix ships, take the field out with a callee that writes the copy back, or copy it (`string_clone`, `vec_clone`) before giving the field a new value.
