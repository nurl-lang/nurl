**agora #449: a closure clearing a local read out of its capture through a `??` payload, a join, a generic identity or an enum payload.** `: e ( vec_at vv 0 ) ( vec_clear e )` in a closure body ends the caller's borrows of `vv`'s elements (agora #353). A `vec_get` payload, a join of two elements, `( idv ( vec_at vv 0 ) )`, or an enum payload of a captured value are not followed, and the borrow is read after the clear. Enum payloads are a hole without closures too. Until the fix ships, clear through the direct form `( vec_clear ( vec_at vv 0 ) )`.
