**agora #382: a panic under `recover` between a holder's store of a
callee local's view and its repoint.** `= w @ W { ( string_data b ) }`
then a call that may panic, then `= w @ W { … }`: the unwind releases `b`
and hands `w` back to the `recover` caller still viewing it, when `W` has
no `% Drop` (#284 covers Drop holders). Until the fix ships, don't call
anything that may panic while an `inout` holder views a local.
