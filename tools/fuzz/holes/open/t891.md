**agora #891: a fresh slice handed to a call that hands its parameter back.** `: [i x ( pick ( pick a f ) F )`, where `pick` returns a fresh slice on some runs and its parameter on others: on the runs the inner call was fresh, its slice is released by no one (a leak). The same holds inside a returned literal. Until the fix ships, bind the inner call's result to a name of its own first.
