**agora #653: a raw `s` string, or a struct with a slice field, under a `;` defer.** `: s raw ( nurl_str_cat … )`, then a `^` on some path, then a defer that reads `raw`: the early return frees the string and the function's cleanup frees it again (double free). The same for a struct holding a slice. A slice bound in an arm and read by a defer in that arm, or bound in an inner scope under the name of an outer slice, leaks. Until the fix ships, register the defer right after the binding it reads, before any `^`.
