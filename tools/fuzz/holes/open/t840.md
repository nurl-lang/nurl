**agora #840: a join cursor handed to a keeping call leaks.** In `: ~ String cur ? == k 1 a b  ( vec_push [String] d cur )`, the push copies the cursor's value, but the call path still hands the join's sources over (mem_note_kept_arg / mem_udrop_alias_move_copied). One of `a` and `b` is then never released: 48 bytes under strict LSan. #824 fixed the literal and field versions. The two other shapes are closed: a `: ~` cursor of a `??` payload stored in its arm, and a struct cursor given a field and then stored. Until the fix ships, push a copy, `( mem_dup cur )`, or the source by its own name.
