**agora #483: a closure literal capturing a `vec_at` binding's element.** A closure that captures `: String e ( vec_at [String] t 0 )` and reads `( string_data e )` keeps no tie to `t`: its captures keep their handle copies but not the element-only sources (`__src_handles_only`), so the closure can be kept and called after `t` is released, and it reads freed memory. (The same capture of a `~` loop's element is refused: the element is a part of `t`.) Until the fix ships, do not keep a closure that reads a lent element past its container.
