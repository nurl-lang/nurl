**agora #848: a struct whose only owning field is a slice is released only as a binding of its own.** `: H { [String names i n }`: an H stored in a Vec, a slice, another struct or an enum payload never frees `names` (or its Strings), because such a struct has no drop by type. A bound H literal frees it on its way out. Until the fix ships, keep such a struct in a binding of its own, or give it a `( Vec T )` field in place of the slice.
