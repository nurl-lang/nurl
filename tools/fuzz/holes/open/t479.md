**agora #479: a copied closure that views a local, a closure body handing a `sink` a value derived from its capture, and a statement-form match arm returning a closure over a borrowed field.** `( mem_dup c )` of a bound closure that views a local outlives the local. A closure body that hands a `sink` a local copy of its capture, a part read out of a capture's payload through a field, a vec element or a borrowed parameter, or an inout capture, frees it twice on a second call or at the env's drop. `?? o { T s → { ^ \ → … s … } }` with `o` a parameter's field frees the caller's field twice. Until the fix ships, copy a capture before handing it to a `sink` in the body (`( eat ( string_clone x ) )`), return the closure literal as the arm's value rather than from a block, and don't copy a closure that views a local.
