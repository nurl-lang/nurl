**agora #697: an `inout` parameter assigned a field of a borrowed parameter.** `@ refill inout ( Vec i ) dst App x → v { = dst . x v }` stores the borrowed field's handle into the caller's binding uncopied: the caller's Vec (or String) and the lender's field then own one buffer, and growing or releasing one leaves the other reading freed memory (heap-use-after-free in safe, single-threaded code). Assigning the whole by-value parameter (`= dst x`) or a field of the `inout` struct (`= . dst v . x v`) copies and is fine. Until the fix ships, assign a copy — `= dst ( vec_clone [i] . x v )`, `= dst ( string_clone . x name )` — when the value comes out of another parameter's field.
