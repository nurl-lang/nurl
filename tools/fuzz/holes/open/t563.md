**agora #563: a value that views its own field, stored into a container and taken back out.** A struct whose field views another of its fields (`= . o v ( slice_from_vec . o buf )`), pushed into a Vec (`( vec_push vs o )`) and taken back out (`vec_pop`), then grown (`vec_push . y buf`), leaves its view reading freed memory (heap-use-after-free). The container's elements carry the view, but nothing ties the value taken out to its own field again. Until the fix ships, keep a value's views of its own storage out of values stored in containers, or take the view again after taking the value out.
