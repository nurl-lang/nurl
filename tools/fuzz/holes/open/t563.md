**agora #563: a value that views its own field, moved out of its frame or passed as a temporary literal.** A struct whose field views another of its fields (`= . o v ( slice_from_vec [u] . o buf )`) and is then moved (`@ P { o 0 }`, pushed and popped, handed to a `sink`) and grown there, or a literal of a storage field and a view passed straight to a call that grows the storage (`( use @ S { sv b } )`), leaves the view reading freed memory (heap-use-after-free). Until the fix ships, keep a value's views of its own storage out of values that move, and bind such a literal before handing it on.
