**agora #405: a defer body that declares a Vec of its own.** `; { : ( Vec
i ) g ( vec_new [i] ) ( vec_push [i] g 7 ) … }` crashes (a segmentation
fault, without any sanitizer) when the function leaves. The defer chain
runs the body at the way out with its locals' slots in a state they do
not have there. Until the fix ships, keep a defer body to calls and
assignments of bindings declared outside it, or put the work in a block
before the way out.
