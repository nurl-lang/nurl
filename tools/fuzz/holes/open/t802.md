**agora #802: a returned closure's effects are lost in some spellings.** A closure a function hands back carries what it does to the arguments it captured to the caller's `:` binding — but not when the builder is declared below its caller or is generic, when the argument is a field path or a lending call (`( mk . w xs )`, `( mk ( vec_at vv 0 ) )`), when the return is a join or a closure handed back through another call, when the result is given with `=`, or when a by-reference capture is re-pointed after the literal. Running it then leaves the caller's view dangling (heap-use-after-free). Until the fix ships, take views of what such a closure captured after running it.
