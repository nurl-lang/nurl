**agora #726: a view of an Rc's payload taken through a Vec of shares, kept past a change through another handle of that Rc.** With `hs` a `( Vec ( Rc ( Vec u ) ) )` holding a clone of `r`, a slice of `( rc_get ( vec_at hs 0 ) )` dangles once `r` is handed to a helper that clears and shrinks the payload (heap-use-after-free): the view's source is `hs`, and a Vec holds its shares in its buffer, not in its own storage. Until the fix ships, take views of a payload through the Rc handle itself (`( rc_get h )` of a bound share), not through the container that holds it.
