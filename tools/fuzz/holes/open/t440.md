**agora #440: a copy of an owner's handle, made by a route that is not
followed, then grown.** A copy made by a call that may lend either of two
arguments (`: ~ String o ( pick x other k )`), by `=` instead of `:` (`= c
( id b )`), or by a callee that lends a field (`( name_of hs )`, `^ . h
name`), grown with `string_push_str` or `vec_reserve`, ends no view of the
owner's buffer, so a view taken before reads freed memory. Until the fix
ships, grow a String or Vec only through its owner's own name while a view
of it is live.
