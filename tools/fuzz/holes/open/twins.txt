# Elaborated twins known to fault (tools/fuzz/holes/elab.py, Agora #242).
#
# Each line names a probe whose twin — the producer form bound by `:` first,
# the consumer reading the binding — is still a hole on main, though the
# probe itself is closed. They share one gap: a binding does not inherit
# what its producer lends (a call in an arm lending a local, a field or a
# view of an inner local, a value a `;` defer releases), so handing the
# binding on is not judged as handing the form on is. That is what #222's
# elaboration closes at the source; each stage takes its lines out of here.
#
# probe                                                   what the twin does
defer_reads_block_returned.nu                             a block's tail `l`, bound then handed back, read by a `;` defer after the caller has it
defer_releases_block_returned.nu                          the same with the defer handing `l` to a `sink`: the probe is rejected, its twin is not
drop_join_arm_result_returned.nu                          a `% Drop` payload a call in an arm lends out of a local, bound then handed back
drop_ret_block_join_local.nu                              a block ending in a join over a `% Drop` local, bound then handed back
drop_vec_get_local_match.nu                               a `vec_get` element of a local Vec through a `??`, bound then handed back
inner_payload_field_through_a_match_assigned_outward.nu   a field of an inner block's enum payload through a `??`, bound then assigned outward
inner_struct_field_through_a_cond_assigned_outward.nu     a field of an inner block's struct through a `?`, bound then assigned outward
inner_view_through_a_cond_assigned_outward.nu             a view of an inner block's struct field through a `?`, bound then assigned outward
raw_view_block_tail.nu                                    a raw view of a block's own local as its tail, bound then handed back
