**agora #450: an owner given a part of its own value while nurlc takes
the result for another value.** `= o ( first o )`, with `first` handing
back an element of o's own Vec field through a generic it calls: o's old
value is released with the part inside it, and reading o reads freed
memory. Until the fix ships, take the part out of the owner into a new
binding (or copy it, `mem_dup`) before giving it to the owner.
