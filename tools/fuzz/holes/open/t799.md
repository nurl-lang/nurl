**agora #799: a view of a binding moved into a struct literal is neither ended nor moved to the field.** `: ( Slice u ) sl ( slice_from_vec xs )` then `: X xx @ X { xs }` then `( vec_reserve . xx v 4096 )` — or handing `xx`, or `@ X { xs }` inline, to a callee that grows the field — leaves `sl` reading the moved buffer (heap-use-after-free). Until the fix ships, take the view from the struct's field after the move, `( slice_from_vec . xx v )`.
