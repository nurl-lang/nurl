**agora #312: a view a closure gives one of its captures, by a route
nurlc does not record.** `= w @ V { ( string_data b ) }` in a closure
body is tracked (#259), but a field store (`= . w v ( string_data b )`),
a push into a captured Vec, a view taken from the closure's own
parameter or from a local, and a run through a copy of the closure, a
struct field or another closure are not: when `b` ends before `w` is
read, or before `w`'s `% Drop` destructor runs, that read is of freed
memory. Until the fix ships, give the view through a function's `inout`
parameter, passing the view itself (`s v`, as #301 says), not from a
closure.
