**agora #664: an owned slice has no drop flag.** Who releases a slice buffer is decided at compile time, so a second name for one handed back (`: [i c a  ^ c`, `^ ? k a b`, `^ . q xs`), a conditional move (`? k { = dst src } {}`) and an assigned join of a fresh slice and a binding release the buffer twice or leave the caller reading freed memory; a bound join's fresh path, a slice from a closure call, slices held by elements or payloads, and a local slice read by a returned slice literal are released by nobody. Until the fix ships, give each slice one owning binding, hand back that binding by name, and copy rather than alias.
