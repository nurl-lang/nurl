**agora #447: a parameter's lent element handed to a clearing callee by an indirect route.** A function handing `( vec_at vv 0 )` of its parameter to a clearing callee has that drop in its summary (agora #443). The element bound to a local first, a `??` payload of `vec_get`, a generic higher-order function, or an element of an element are not followed, so the caller's borrows of the element's contents survive the clear. Until the fix ships, don't hold such borrows across the call.
