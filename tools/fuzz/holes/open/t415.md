**agora #415: a view stored into a Slice.** `( slice_put [s] sl 0 (
string_data loc ) )`, with `sl` a `slice_from_vec` of `vs`, or a
`slice_copy` from a Vec that holds a view, records no dependency at all,
so `vs` (or `sl` itself) read after `loc` ends reads freed memory. Until
the fix ships, store views through the Vec (`vec_set`, `vec_push`), not
through a Slice of it.
