**agora #420: a closure picked by a join, or a closure literal's own closure-typed parameter, then called.** A call through a closure binding answers with what the closures bound to it do (agora #359). A binding given `? c clr nop` (or a block's arms), or a closure literal's closure parameter `g` called as `( g y )`, is read by its interface only, and its plain parameters count as unchanged. So a clearing closure picked that way ends no borrow of the argument's elements: a heap-use-after-free. Until the fix ships, bind each closure by name and call the binding, or pass it to a named function rather than to a closure literal.
