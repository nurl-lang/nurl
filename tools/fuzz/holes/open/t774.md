**agora #774: a payload view written into an `inout` parameter.** A callee that writes a view of `( rc_get ( vec_at hs 0 ) )` into an `inout` parameter leaves the caller's view unmarked, and a reset of the payload through another handle afterwards leaves it reading freed memory. (The `~` loop over an Rc payload of numbers reset through another handle is closed: a call that drops the payload's elements and may move its buffer ends the loop's cursor too.) Until the fix ships, take a view of a payload after the call that changes it.
