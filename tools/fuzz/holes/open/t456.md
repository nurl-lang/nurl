**agora #456: an `inout` binding given a lend of another value that
#438's copy does not reach.** An `inout` Box, HashMap, Rc or Arc given a
call's lend of another parameter, or a `sink` argument handed back into an
`inout` String: the caller's binding ends up sharing another owner's
value, and one of them reads freed memory or leaks. Until the fix ships,
give an `inout` binding a value of its own (`mem_dup` of the lend).
