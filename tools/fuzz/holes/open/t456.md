**agora #456: an `inout` binding given a lend of another value that
#438's copy does not reach.** A local that holds a lend of a borrowed
parameter assigned by name (`: String t ( id other )  = o t`), an `inout`
Box, HashMap, Rc or Arc given a call's lend of another parameter, or a
`sink` argument handed back into an `inout` String: the caller's binding
ends up sharing another owner's value, and one of them reads freed
memory. Until the fix ships, give an `inout` binding a value of its own
(`mem_dup` of the lend).
