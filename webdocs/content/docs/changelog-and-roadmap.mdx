NURL changes quickly. A copy of either document here would be out of date
immediately. Both documents live in the repository. They are the source
of truth:

<Callout type="info" title="Read these in the repo">
- **[`CHANGELOG.md`](https://github.com/nurl-lang/nurl/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)** — the full, reverse-chronological record of what shipped, release by release.
- **[`ROADMAP.md`](https://github.com/nurl-lang/nurl/blob/main/ROADMAP.md)** — a concise, forward-looking snapshot of what's solid today, what's left before 1.0, and what's planned after.
</Callout>

Anything the roadmap marks as done has a regression test in
[`compiler/tests/`](https://github.com/nurl-lang/nurl/tree/main/compiler/tests).
The [bootstrap fixed point](./editor-and-tooling) covers it.
