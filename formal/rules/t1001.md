| An `inout` parameter, and a field of one, is the caller's storage: it outlives every binding of the frame. A stack reference stored into it dangles once the function returns. The escape pass now refuses assigning one to the parameter (`bck_esc_assign`) or storing one into its field (`bck_esc_field_store`), whatever the scope depth of the referenced binding: a closure that captures a local by reference, directly or through a local binding of the closure. Before, on main 21dd7c7a, the target's depth defaulted to the function body's, so a body-level local looked no deeper, and the caller ran the closure through the callee's dead stack slot (ASan: stack-use-after-scope) (AGORA-1001) | §2.3 | h984, h985, h986 | none to the proof: it refuses more programs, and A2 already has no stack reference that outlives its frame |
