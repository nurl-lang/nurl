| A copy of a value that holds views holds the same views (agora #362, #368, #377, #347). What a function makes of what its argument holds is derived from its walk, as #422 derives what it makes of the argument's storage: a parameter that holds views (an owner of them, a Slice, a struct of views) starts its walk holding a pseudo source of its own, `%p`, standing for the caller's views. A value handed back holding them answers `%k` (g_fn_rviews), and another parameter left holding them answers `h>%k` (g_fn_vkeeps; `%K.H` rows at the call). The caller asks what a copy of argument k holds, its views and not its storage (`g_sxe_held`): an owner's dependencies, or what a borrow's sources' elements view. `mem_dup` answers `:%0`, and publishes its operand. A callee with no walk answer (trusted or library code, compiled) whose result can hold views holds what its non-closure arguments hold, and so does a match payload of such a call. A copy taken by the call it is an argument of still holds what it held. A copy of a part of an argument (an element or a field handle, a slice of it; `retpart`) holds what the part holds, not the storage the part is in, and a holder that borrows its value depends on what that value holds, its borrow tying it to the rest. A view field of an owner copied whole (`mem_dup`'s operand, an owner parameter's `%k`, a `%K.H` row: `g_sxe_hctn`) is copied itself, so the copy points where the field does; a view argument itself holds what the elements of its storage view (where it points is its lends'), and a callee that copies the elements of a view it is given — `g_fn_vcopies`, a parameter that is a view of a container (`g_fn_eparams`) — holds those. What a parameter holds given to a string global or another thread is refused in the function, as a view parameter's own storage is (unless the parameter escapes there whole, its caller then asked), and a bare view handed back holding it answers `%k`. A parameter the body may give a new value (`inout`, owned) holds `%p` only on the paths that do not: `%p` is a dependency the joins and a loop's dependency snapshot follow. A callee with no walk answer of views (trusted, library or `unsafe` code, compiled) leaves each `inout` argument whose type holds views holding what its non-closure arguments hold (`h>%k`).
