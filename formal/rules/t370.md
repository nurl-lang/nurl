| A view the caller passed — a parameter that is neither a `sink` nor `inout`, of a type that holds addresses (an `s`, a slice, a struct of views; `~name`, the walk's `pv_`) — stands for what it points into: stored into a holder parameter (directly, through a borrow of it, or by a callee that keeps it), the holder depends on it, the callee's answer names the pair h>k, and the caller ties the argument at h to the view argument's sources. Kept by a callee, such a parameter is no move: the callee keeps a copy of the address. Before, a view parameter had no sources in its function, so the caller tied the view to the callee's first argument (#259) whatever holder the callee stored it in (agora #370, #397) | §2.10 | yes: made to reject more (a view kept in a holder other than the first argument outlived its source); a view parameter handed to a keeping callee is no longer moved, which accepts a read of it afterwards | none to the proof: nurlc brought into line with A2, where a view argument's R entries go to the holder outHolds names, and a copy of a view moves nothing |
