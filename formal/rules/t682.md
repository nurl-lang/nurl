| A view of an Arc's payload stays one through the owners it is kept in (agora #682): a Vec it is pushed into, a map it is put in, a struct a Vec holds, a struct a helper builds over its Arc parameter. Such an owner is marked holding one (`ah_`, set by the store, by an owner bound to a value holding one, and by a copy of it). What is lent back out of it is one: a `??` payload, a value a call lends out of it whose type can hold a view (`vec_at`, `vec_get`, `map_get`, a helper's element), a field of it, a borrow of it. The frozen-payload rule then refuses a change through it, as for the view bound directly. A struct's view field given one after the struct was built makes the struct one (`ab_`), as its literal does.
