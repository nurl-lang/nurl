| A closure read out of a parameter's storage through a pointer (`vec_at`'s `^ . data idx` over its Vec parameter: raw provenance) is handed back LENT, as a String read so is: no copy of its environment. The function's `@.__nurl_retclo` constant says "owned" unless every closure `^` it has lends, and a lending path copies only where the constant says owned. At a direct call the caller's binding takes the env only where the constant says owned (`select(own, env, null)`) — and only to a `:` binding that is the whole call of a library (or `unsafe`) function, in safe code, out of Vecs the frame owns by name (`__clo_lend_args_local`, `g_clo_rhs_at`); any other read is copied at the call (`__clo_tmp_settle`), since the walk does not follow a Vec reached through an Rc's second handle (agora #345) or check views in `unsafe` code — so `: f ( vec_at fs i )`, fs a local, is a borrow of fs, as spec §8.4 has always said. The binding keeps the lend only while every use of it is one the walk orders exactly: a direct call `( f … )`, at the binding's closure depth, while no call's effect is still waiting to be recorded (a statement's call effects are recorded after its reads, bck_flush_moves: an earlier call in the statement, a call in f's own arguments, or a call in an enclosing `?` / `~` header). Any other use — read as a value (an argument, `: g f`, `{ f }`, a join), captured by a closure — or a call in such a window or with a call completed while its own arguments were compiled (g_calls_total: a value block, arm or match in the arguments flushes), or a call after its Vec was moved, stored into an owner (a literal's field, an element: bck_stash_store) or handed to a call that takes it over (sink, kept, escaped — the walk's own `pendcall` verdict at module end, __clolend_final; the walk re-roots the borrow into a handle it does not follow, `( rc_new fs )`, agora #345), makes the binding copy what was lent at its binding (`@.__nurl_clolend.K`, resolved at module end, g_clolk_n), as before #552; it stays a borrow of fs in the walk either way. The walk ends it when fs changes (cleared, freed, an element replaced; growth moves no env). Every other consumer — a join, an argument, `^`, a store into an owner, a `sink` parameter, a spawn — gets a copy where it was lent (`__clo_tmp_settle`), and the walk's summaries are unchanged.
