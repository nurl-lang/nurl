| A struct or option that owns something, read OUT of a parameter, now makes a drop edge on that parameter when handed to a callee that drops through it. Three cases: an element bound first (`: H e ( vec_at [H] hs 0 )  ( wipe e )`), a `~` loop's element (`~ w ws { ( wipe w ) }`), and a field of a parameter read whole (`: P e . q p`). The edge carries the callee's own field path, as the inline `( wipe ( vec_at [H] hs 0 ) )` already did (`drops=0.rows`). A field read whole keeps the path it was read at (`__pdpath`), so `( wipe . e h )` drops at `0.p.h.rows`, and a borrow of a sibling field (`q.p.g`) stays. A field of a struct reached some other way (`. cl transport` of `( rc_get . . call client a )`) stays unfollowed, because its edge would name no path into the parameter. On main fd008190 these were in no summary, and a caller's view into the cleared field dangled after the call.
