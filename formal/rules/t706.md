| A closure binding declared a write-back (`mem_put_back`, a raw primitive) is stored into an element slot as it is, as a binding of every other type is: it was read out of storage and returns to it, in its own slot or another (two elements changing places). Before, a closure element store copied every binding's environment (`mem_clo_into_store`), `mem_put_back` or not, so a container moving closures through a binding (a `HashMap`'s rehash, `vec_swap`, `vec_reverse`, `vec_retain`, `sort_by`, a `Heap`'s sift) left a clone in the new slot and the original environment with no owner: a leak, one per element moved. The library's moves declare their elements write-backs now.
