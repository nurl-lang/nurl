| `vec_retain` judges every element before it drops one. The kept elements are swapped to the front, slot for slot, so the `Vec` holds each of its elements exactly once whenever the predicate runs; then the length goes down to the kept ones and the others are dropped through `mem_drop_range` (`nurl_vec_drop_range_dtor`, the record `vec_truncate` and `vec_clear` use). A predicate that panics leaves a `Vec` the drain drops once per element; a destructor that panics leaves a `Vec` of the kept elements, and the others still to go are dropped all the same. Before, each element that went was dropped inside the loop with the `Vec` at its old length, and a panic's drain dropped the `Vec` over slots already given up.
