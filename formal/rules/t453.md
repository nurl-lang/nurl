| A loop whose condition is the literal `T` (or a non-zero integer literal) is never left from its head: where it has a `break`, it is left only by its `break`s, so its exit state, borrow sources and dependencies are theirs (the dependency tables are put back to the union of the `break`s' snapshots, `__vd_restore`), not joined with the head's. A loop with no `break` keeps the head join (it is never left; nothing after it runs).
