Where the function hands its value on, by name (#778) or as the join arm that selects it (`( eat ? c p ( mk ) )`), the caller's slot is emptied on those runs, and the function is summarised as replacing the parameter (`__replaces_note`), so every borrow and view the caller holds of it ends at the call; a block arm lends it and the callee is handed a copy. The caller drops its binding as ever, and may read it after the call.
