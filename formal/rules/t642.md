| Three struct-literal rules (agora #642). (1) A closure field of a struct with a drop of its own — a `% Drop`, or the compiler-written drop of a struct holding such a value — is released by that drop with the rest; it is no longer registered for a release of its own besides (`__agg_drop_takes_fields`), which freed the env twice at the binding's end and when the struct moved through a `Vec`. (2) A struct literal handed straight to a parameter that only reads it owns the `% Drop` fields it made, as it owned its handle fields: they are dropped after the call unless the callee keeps or consumes the argument. (3) A field the literal made (a call's value, a literal, a closure) is journaled until the literal is complete (`__agg_field_journal`), so a later field's panic releases it. Accepts more, two shapes main refused only because of (1)'s second registration: such a struct moved to another binding (`: C b a`; main: "owns raw string or slice fields that are freed when 'a' goes out of scope") and handed to a `sink` parameter (main: "a compiler-auto-dropped value; passing it to a 'sink' parameter is not yet supported"); with one owner for the env they are the ordinary moves of a struct with a drop of its own, each run clean under ASan + strict LSan. Nothing is newly refused.
