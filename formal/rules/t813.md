| A returned closure's effects reach the caller's binding of the result, and are replayed where it runs, in two more shapes. First, when its builder's answer (`retcloeff`) is not there where the call is: the builder is declared below its caller or is a generic instance. The binding gets one placeholder per argument (`__rce_pending`, `__rce_placeholders`), and a run of it is a `pendrce` row. The walk is parked until the module has compiled (a pending row), and then expands that row into the `pendeff` / `pendlend` rows the builder's final answer gives its parameter (`__bck_rce_expand`), as a builder above its caller does. A function that hands such a closure back carries the placeholders in its own answer. Second, when the closure comes back through a callee that returns its argument (`@ pick ( @ v ) f → ( @ v ) { ^ f }`, `retclo`): the closure handed in has its effects (`__ce_by_arg`) passed to the result. A closure that only reads stays accepted: an answer with nothing for the parameter expands to no row.
