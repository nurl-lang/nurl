| A `:` binding of a `?` join whose arm reads a field of a struct (`: t ? c . p s ( mk )`) borrows that field. Codegen neither takes nor copies the field for it, as it does for `: x . p s` and a value block's tail, so the walk marks the borrow `bxf_`, and the struct's or the matched binding's end ends it: the borrow itself, whatever takes its handle (`= t __el`, a `maybealias` handover, a `vcborrow`), and one that may own its value on the other paths (BCK_MAYBE_OWNED). A `??` arm reading a field of the matched payload stays open (open/t242.md): the walk's source expression names the matched binding there, not the field, and cannot tell it from a payload taken out whole (`T v → v`), which owns its value.
