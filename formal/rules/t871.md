| Early exits in the calculus: `break`, `continue` and a `^` before the end of a function's body are statements of A2 (`Stmt.block`, `Stmt.exit n`, `Stmt.loop` for `while T`). An `exit n` leaves `n + 1` blocks in one step (`ExitAt`, `Step.blockExit`), ending every scope it leaves as that scope's own end does, its drops and destructors run; typing carries the exits a statement may take (`StmtT`'s `Exits` index: `Exits.under`, `Exits.underD`, `Exits.blk`), and a block's end says no more than the context each exit leaves from.
