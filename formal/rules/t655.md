| A `;` defer inside a loop body: what it keeps, and when a kept binding is released (agora #655). A defer runs once, at function exit, if its `;` was reached in any round, so a way out above the `;` meets a defer an earlier round armed. Inside the outermost loop whose body registers a defer, (1) the owners in sight at the loop's head are in the defer snapshots from there on (`__dloop_open`), (2) a `:` binding with a `;` further down its own block is in them from its binding on, and before this round's value is stored the slot's value from an earlier round is released where that round left it to the cleanup and the binding still owned it (`mem_dkeep_rebind`, `__dkr_expand`: its "left to the cleanup" flag and its drop flag; a closure's environment under its owner slot), and (3) a `^` or `\` enters the chain through the loop's label, at the chain's top as the loop's end has it.
