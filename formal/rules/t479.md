| In a closure body, a value handed to a `sink` that is derived from one of the closure's captures (`__clo_cap_source`) is refused. That covers a `:` copy of the capture's whole value, or a copy of such a copy however deep (`__capcopy`, or its slot's `__alias`), a payload or a view of one (`__plroot`, `__lvroot`), and a part or handle copy (`__clo_nc_derived`), to the end of the chain. The environment still holds that value and releases it, so it would be released twice: on a second call, or at the environment's drop. The capture itself handed to a sink stays the run-once case (the closure frees it when it runs), and so does a payload read straight out of a capture the body may take over (agora #463's takeover), and a `~` loop's element itself (`__econt`): the loop moves it out of the captured container, which then no longer holds it (agora #588). A payload or a copy of such an element moves nothing, and is refused. That takeover now admits only the frame's own captures: a parameter the function does not take over (borrowed or `inout`) is the caller's, and taking its payload released it under the caller.
